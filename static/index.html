Our process

Exact scope and schedule depend on your project.

  1. Project enquiry

    You tell us about your business, goals and the features you expect.

  2. Discovery and scope

    We review your answers, ask about anything missing and define the scope.

  3. Proposal and agreement

    We agree deliverables, a project-specific quote, timeline and terms before work begins.

  4. Design

    Visual direction, layout, typography and user experience.

  5. Development

    Responsive build with the agreed interactions, forms and functionality.

  6. Testing and refinement

    Checks across screen sizes, navigation, forms, accessibility and performance.

  7. Delivery

    We hand over the agreed website and explain any handover steps.
